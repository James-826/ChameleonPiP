隐私政策（Privacy Policy）

生效日期：2026-11-19 · 适用范围：ChameleonPiP Chrome/Edge 浏览器扩展、macOS/Windows 桌面端及联机排行榜服务

1. 核心承诺：视频画面与音频 100% 本地处理

ChameleonPiP 是一款专注于光学透明画中画（Picture-in-Picture）体验的桌面与浏览器工具。 我们绝不上传、存储、分析或向任何第三方传输您观看的视频画面、网页内容、音频、字幕或屏幕截图。

  • 浏览器扩展通过 tabCapture 捕获的视频帧仅在您的本机回环地址（ws://127.0.0.1:51937）传输至您本地运行的桌面端进行 Metal/WebGL 实时光学渲染，数据从不离开您的电脑。
  • 桌面端使用 macOS ScreenCaptureKit 采样底层背景亮度时，仅在本地内存计算平均 RGB 亮度值并立即丢弃像素，绝不写入磁盘或网络发送。

2. 浏览器扩展权限用途说明

  • tabCapture & offscreen：仅在您主动点击投送或按下快捷键（⌃⇧G）后，将当前标签页的视频区域渲染到本机画中画窗口。
  • activeTab & scripting：定位当前网页中的 <video> 元素坐标、同步播放/暂停/进度/字幕控制。
  • tabs：实现低延迟标签页级静音切换以及跨标签页悬浮控制。
  • storage：在本地保存您的投影模式、浓度、羽化、悬停开关等个人偏好设置。

3. 排行榜与会员激活所涉及的最小化数据

当您主动使用全球/同城排行榜、1v1 耐力 PK 或 Pro 会员激活码功能时，服务端仅保存以下最小化必要字段：

  • 您自定义的匿名代号（Nickname）、自选城市/地区标签、行业标签。
  • 真实视频播放累计时长、连续纪录、PK 胜负场次与 ELO 积分。
  • 随机生成的匿名设备标识（userId / deviceId）与激活码授权状态（用于防止单码超限多设备滥用）。

我们不收集您的真实姓名、邮箱、浏览历史、Cookie、密码或任何个人身份敏感信息（PII），也不会向广告商出售或共享任何用户数据。

4. 数据删除与联系方式

您可随时在浏览器本地存储中清除扩展数据，或卸载扩展与桌面端以停止一切数据同步。 如需清除云端匿名排行榜记录或咨询隐私问题，请通过 GitHub Issues（https://github.com/James-826/ChameleonPiP）或邮件联系我们。

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 · Applies to ChameleonPiP Chrome/Edge Extension, macOS/Windows Desktop App, and Arena Services

1. Zero Media Upload Guarantee (100% Local Processing)

ChameleonPiP is an optical Picture-in-Picture (PiP) productivity and viewing tool. We never upload, record, store, inspect, or share your video frames, audio streams, webpage contents, subtitles, or screen captures with any external server or third party.

  • Video frames captured via tabCapture are streamed strictly over your machine's local loopback interface (ws://127.0.0.1:51937) to your local desktop window for real-time WebGL/Metal rendering and never leave your device.
  • Ambient color sampling (e.g., via macOS ScreenCaptureKit) computes an in-memory average RGB value locally and discards the sample immediately; nothing is saved to disk or transmitted over the network.

2. Browser Extension Permissions Justification

  • tabCapture & offscreen: Invoked strictly upon explicit user action (clicking the extension button or pressing ⌃⇧G) to relay the active video region to your local PiP window.
  • activeTab & scripting: Used solely to detect the <video> bounding box, read optional subtitles, and execute play/pause/seek/rate commands you trigger.
  • tabs: Used to provide instant tab-level mute/unmute synchronization and detect when a streaming tab closes.
  • storage: Stores your local visual preferences (projection mode, intensity, feathering, hover-reveal toggle) and membership license status.

3. Minimal Data Used for Optional Leaderboard & Pro Licensing

Only when you use the optional Leaderboard / PK Arena or Pro License Key activation, our server stores the following minimal non-PII fields:

  • A self-chosen anonymous nickname, self-selected city/region tag, and optional industry tag.
  • Active video playback timer totals, streak records, and PK ELO score.
  • A randomly generated anonymous identifier (userId / deviceId) and license entitlement metadata to enforce per-license device limits.

We do not collect browsing history, cookies, passwords, payment card details, or personally identifiable information (PII). We do not sell user data or serve third-party ads.

4. Data Deletion & Contact

You may clear local extension storage or uninstall ChameleonPiP at any time. To request removal of an anonymous leaderboard entry or ask privacy questions, please open an issue at https://github.com/James-826/ChameleonPiP or contact our support team.
